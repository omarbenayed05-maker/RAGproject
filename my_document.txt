**Q: What is the Moonberry Café?**
A: Moonberry Café is a tiny café in the fictional town of Bellweather, famous for its glowing blueberry pies.

**Q: When does the café open?**
A: It opens at sunrise and closes whenever the owner, Mira, decides she has baked enough cookies.

**Q: Can I bring my pet?**
A: Yes. Cats, dogs, and even well-behaved dragons are welcome.

**Q: What is the café’s most popular drink?**
A: The Stardust Latte, made with cinnamon, honey, and a mysterious silver syrup.

**Q: Is the silver syrup magical?**
A: Mira insists it is only flavored sugar, but customers claim it makes their dreams unusually vivid.

**Q: Do you take reservations?**
A: Only for parties of six or more—or for anyone who arrives carrying a birthday cake.

**Q: Where can I find the café?**
A: Follow the cobblestone road past the old clock tower, then turn left when you smell fresh cinnamon.

**Q: Is Moonberry Café a real place?**
A: No. But if you find it, please let us know.
